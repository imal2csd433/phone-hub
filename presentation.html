How to Use — Step-by-Step Operating Guide

Works on iOS, Android, Windows & macOS

Step 1: Open & Sign In

Open the web link on any smartphone or PC browser. Log in with your assigned account:
• Owner / Admin: Full edit rights
• Viewer: Search & View only

Step 2: Search by SN or Phone

In the top search bar, type the device's Serial Number (SN), SIM Phone Number, or Employee Name. The matching device card appears instantly.

Step 3: View or Edit Topics

Tap the device card to open full details across 3 structured sections (Device Info, Custodian, Additional Specs/Notes). Admins/Owners can edit and click Save.

Step 4: Print or Export PDF

Tap "Print Handover Sheet" on any device record. The system strips away navigation buttons and formats a clean A4/PDF document ready for signing or filing.
